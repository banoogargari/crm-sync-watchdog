## Issues

### 1. Customer Summit never reached the CRM

_Campaign missing in CRM: A program ran in the marketing tool but never reached the CRM, so its attendees' activity is invisible to attribution._

**Opportunities:** 5 · **Pipeline:** $210,000 · **Won ARR:** $0 · **Needs review**

- **What happened:** The Q3 2026 Customer Summit (event on 2026-08-06, 35 members, 29 of them successful) exists in the marketing tool but there is no matching campaign in the CRM. Attendance was therefore never recorded there, and five deals are credited to whatever earlier activity the contact had.
- **Impact:** Five opportunities worth $210,000 of pipeline are in the wrong channel. If fixed, Events rises from $12,000 to $246,000 of pipeline. 6sense loses O5225 ($60,000). Content Syndication loses O5262 ($30,000). LinkedIn loses O5266 ($24,000). Organic loses O5238 ($36,000). No marketing activity loses O5234 ($60,000). Example: O5225 (Summit Systems) is shown as 6sense from a form fill on 2026-07-26, but the contact attended the Summit on 2026-08-06, which is the true last touch.
- **Recommended action:** Marketing ops should create or link a CRM campaign for the Q3 2026 Customer Summit and sync its 35 members, then re-run the report. Also find out why the event campaign wasn't created automatically.

### 3. Form lost ad tracking

_Form lost ad tracking: Form fills arrive without their source (UTM), so the deal shows "No source" even when the person came from an ad._

**Opportunities:** 7 · **Pipeline:** $288,000 · **Won ARR:** $0 · **Needs review**

No source would still hold $186,000 of pipeline after the fix.

A person should review the three click-ID cases before crediting them, and look at the four unresolved ones by hand. The ABM hints are not to be credited automatically.
