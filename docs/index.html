How to read this report
Reported today
What the CRM shows right now: the number on today's dashboard.
If fixes are approved
What it would be once a person approves the proposed fixes. Nothing has been changed yet.
Trusted / Not trusted
Not trusted means at least one data problem moves money into or out of that channel; the problems are listed under it.
Sourced vs. influenced
Sourced: the channel of the last marketing touch before the deal was created (each deal counted once). Influenced: programs the account engaged with along the way (a deal can count for several).
Campaign missing in CRM
A program ran in the marketing tool but never reached the CRM, so its attendees' activity is invisible to attribution.
Attendance not updated in CRM
The marketing tool shows people attended, but the CRM still says "Registered", so the activity does not count.
Form lost ad tracking
Form fills arrive without their source (UTM), so the deal shows "No source" even when the person came from an ad.
Duplicate contacts
The same person exists twice; the deal sits on the copy with no marketing history.
Deal with no contact
Sales created the opportunity without a contact, so it cannot be traced to any marketing activity.
Deal amount ≠ billing
The won deal amount in the CRM differs from what billing actually charges (billing is correct).
